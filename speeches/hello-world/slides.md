## MathJax Support (公式渲染)

The build script will seamlessly bundle and render complex LaTeX math formulas via **MathJax**:

When $a \ne 0$, there are two solutions to \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\) and they are:

$$ x = {-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac} \over 2a} $$

::: notes
MathJax is loaded locally so it works even completely offline during your speech!
:::
